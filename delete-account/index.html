Delete Your Purple One Account

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Purple One account (by Purple World Technology) and what happens to your data. Only the business owner can delete the account.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open Purple One and sign in as the business owner.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Read the message, enter your password and confirm.

You are signed out on every device straight away and the account is locked.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, write from the email address registered on the account to support@purpleworldtechnology.com with the subject Delete my Purple One account. Please mention your shop name and registered phone number. We confirm your identity and then start the deletion.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, phone number, password and PIN, and the logins of your staff.
  • Your shop profile (address, UPI, bank details), customer and supplier contact details, and employee personal details (phone, address, bank, PAN and similar).
  • Notification tokens and sign-in / device history.

All of this is erased within 30 days of your request.

What we must keep

Tax invoices and related accounting records are kept for 8 years. Indian GST and income-tax law require these records to be retained. They are used only for legal and audit purposes, are not linked to any sign-in, and are removed after 8 years.

Changed your mind?

During the 30 days you can write to support@purpleworldtechnology.com and ask us to cancel the deletion. After the 30 days the personal data cannot be recovered. Please take a backup or export of your reports before you delete.
