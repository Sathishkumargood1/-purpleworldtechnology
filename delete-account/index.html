Delete your Purple One account and data

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Purple One (Android package com.purple_world_technology.purpleone) is made by Purple World Technology, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India. This page explains how to delete a Purple One account and the data that belongs to it.

Who can delete what

  • Business owner: can delete the whole business account (steps below).
  • Staff with app access: ask the owner to remove your access, or write to us (see "Staff, employees, customers and suppliers").
  • Employees, customers, suppliers: your data was entered by a business. See the last section below.

Option 1: delete inside the app (owner)

  1. Sign in as the business owner.
  2. Open Settings, then Delete Account.
  3. Read the message, enter your password and confirm.

You are signed out on every device at once and the account is locked. There is no cancel button in the app. To cancel, follow "Changed your mind?" below.

Option 2: you cannot open the app (owner)

Write to support@purpleworldtechnology.com from the email address registered on the account. Use the subject Delete my Purple One account and include your shop name and registered phone number. We check that the request is really from you (we may send a code to your registered email or phone). We then start the deletion within 3 working days and tell you by email.

What happens, and when

WhenWhat happens
Day 0The account is locked and every sign-in on every device ends
Days 1 to 30Waiting period. Nothing is erased yet, so a request made by mistake can be undone
After day 30Personal data is erased by our daily clean-up, usually within a day or two after the 30th day

What is erased

  • Shop profile: phone, email, address, UPI ID, bank details and helpline. The shop name becomes "Deleted business".
  • Customers and suppliers: phone numbers, email, PAN, addresses, contact person, bank details and notes.
  • Employees: name, phone, email, date of birth, address, emergency contact, bank account, IFSC, UPI, PAN, PF and ESI numbers. The salary amount is cleared (set to zero).
  • Staff logins and memberships, and the passwords, PINs, sessions, push tokens, sign-in history and device history linked to them.
  • Personal details inside older sync records.
  • Name, email and phone of any person who belongs to no other Purple One business.

What we keep, and why

  • Tax invoices, bills, ledger entries and the audit log: 8 years, or longer where the law or a legal case requires. We keep them to meet GST and income-tax record-keeping rules. They carry no sign-in details. They keep the names and GSTIN printed on the invoice. We use them only for legal and audit purposes, and remove them when that period ends.
  • Encrypted backups: erased data can remain inside encrypted backup files until those files expire (daily copies 14 days, weekly 35 days, monthly and yearly copies up to about 13 months). Backups are used only to recover from a server failure and cannot be edited. If one is ever restored, we erase the deleted accounts again before anyone can use it.
  • Free-trial fingerprint: a one-way hash of the phone's Android ID, kept so the same phone cannot start a repeat free trial. It is still personal data. We keep it only to stop repeat free trials, never for advertising. If you think a trial was refused by mistake, write to us and we will review it.

Before you delete

  • Export what you need. After the waiting period your data cannot be recovered. Export your reports, GST reports and the backup file from the app first.
  • Your plan is separate. Plans are bought from us or an authorised distributor outside the app and website, and they are not Google Play subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel, settle or refund a plan. Contact us about any plan, renewal or refund.

Changed your mind?

Write to support@purpleworldtechnology.com from the registered email within the 30 days and ask us to cancel the deletion. After 30 days the personal data is gone and cannot be brought back.

Staff, employees, customers and suppliers

If your data is held in a business's Purple One account, that business decides what it keeps. You can ask the business owner to correct or erase it, or write to support@purpleworldtechnology.com from your registered email with the business name and what you want erased. We will pass the request to the owner, help with the erasure, and reply within 30 days. Staff with app access who want their own login removed can write to us the same way.

Delete only some data

Owners can also erase individual customers, suppliers or employees inside the app without deleting the whole account. Records tied to tax invoices are kept for the legal period as described above.
