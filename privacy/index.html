2. Our role: who decides what happens to the data

We store and process it on the owner's behalf, only to run the service. The owner is responsible for having the right to enter that data and for telling their own customers and employees about it.

If you are an employee, customer or supplier of a business that uses Purple One, that business is your first contact for anything about your data. You can also write to us (section 14).

4A. Using the Purple One web viewer

The web viewer is a read-only page where the owner, and staff the owner has allowed, sign in to see bills and reports. It uses the same account data as the app (email, password, PIN) and records your IP address and sign-in time for security. It keeps you signed in with a secure, HTTP-only cookie that ends after a period of inactivity. It uses no advertising cookies, no trackers, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and it stores no business data in your browser. Everything this policy says about security, retention, your rights and deletion applies to the web viewer in the same way.

7. Where data is stored

Our main servers are in India. Encrypted backups are held at Cloudflare R2. Some providers (Google, Brevo) may process the limited data above outside India. We use them only where the law allows such transfer.

8. How we protect data

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) for every connection.
  • The database on your phone is encrypted (SQLCipher) and sits behind your PIN. Sign-in tokens are kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Passwords and PINs are stored only as hashes. Sign-in, PIN and OTP attempts are rate-limited.
  • Screens with private data (PIN, salary, bank details) block screenshots and screen recording.
  • Each business's data is separated at the database level (row-level security). Admin access needs two-factor sign-in.
  • Backups are encrypted before they leave our server, and restore is restricted to our team.
  • Sensitive actions are written to an append-only audit log.

While we use reasonable security measures, no system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You can ask us for the following today. Under the DPDP Act, most of these become legal rights from 13 May 2027, and we are preparing for that date:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct or complete it, or to erase it;
  • have your grievance heard and answered;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act.

Owners can correct most data inside the app. For anything else, write to us (section 14). We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once its complaint process is available to you.

11. Deleting your account

The business owner can delete the account in the app (Settings, Delete Account) or on the web page https://purpleworldtechnology.com/delete-account/. What is erased, what must be kept and why is explained on that page. Staff members should ask the owner or write to us.

12. Children

Purple One is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will erase it.
