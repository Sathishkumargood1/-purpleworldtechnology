Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Purple World Technology ("we", "us") makes Purple One, an Android app for billing, stock, accounts and staff management used by small and medium businesses in India. This page explains what information the app and our servers keep, why, for how long, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Information we keep

WhatWhy
Account details: your name, email address, phone number and a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or PIN hashTo create your account, sign you in and let you reset your password.
Business details you type in: shop name, address, GSTIN, UPI ID, bank details printed on your invoiceTo print your own invoices and run your business in the app.
Business records: bills, invoices, products, stock, customers and suppliers (name, phone, address, GSTIN), employees (name, phone, salary, attendance), payments and ledgersThis is the work you do in the app. It is stored on your phone and synced to our server so you can use several devices and so it is not lost if a phone is lost.
Staff you invite: name, email, phone, roleTo give them their own sign-in with the permissions you choose.
Device and security data: a device identifier, device name, IP address, sign-in time, activation key usedTo keep sessions safe, limit the number of devices per plan and detect misuse.
Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)To send you in-app alerts such as reminders and account notices.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, SMS, call logs or precise location. We do not show advertising and we do not sell your information.

2. Bluetooth, USB and notifications

The app asks for Bluetooth permission only to find and connect to your receipt printer or weighing scale, and USB access only if you connect a USB device. Notification permission is used only to show you alerts. Data from printers and scales is used inside the app and is not sent to us separately.

3. Who we share it with

  • Service providers that run our servers, email delivery and push messages (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging). They process data only to provide the service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • Your business data is visible only to the people you add to your own business. One business can never see another business's data.

4. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. The database on your phone is encrypted. Passwords and PINs are stored only as hashes. Access to each business's data is separated on the server and every request is checked against your sign-in.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your account is active, we keep your data so the app works.
  • If you delete your account, your personal data is erased within 30 days (see section 6).
  • Tax invoices and related accounting records are kept for 8 years after deletion, because Indian GST and income-tax law require businesses and their service providers to keep them. After 8 years they are removed.
  • Security and sign-in history is kept for a limited period and then removed automatically.

6. Deleting your account and data

The business owner can delete the account inside the app (Settings → Delete Account) or from our Delete Account page. Within 30 days we erase the personal data listed in section 1, except the invoices and accounting records that we must keep as described above. Staff members who want their own details removed can ask the business owner or write to us.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information at any time by writing to support@purpleworldtechnology.com. We reply within 7 working days.

8. Children

Purple One is for businesses and is not meant for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

10. This website

The website purpleworldtechnology.com uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the pages (for example clicks and scrolling). It may set cookies and records usage data that does not include your name or business records. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a script library from Cloudflare cdnjs, which means those providers can see your IP address. The Purple One app does not use Microsoft Clarity. If you do not want website analytics, you can block cookies in your browser.
